Three real projects.

Each one told the same way: the problem, what I did, what changed. Client names are kept private.

Global shipping company · 2026

231hidden copies of one silent mistake, found by searching for its whole family

ABuiltI did the engineering myself

The invoice robot that was quietly getting sums wrong.

The problem
A robot that makes invoices in 37 countries had been moved from one automation tool to another. Only about 1 in 10 of its 337 parts had ever been checked.
What I did
I ran it for real instead of just reading the code. In three weeks I found and fixed 20 mistakes, and noticed they were only 9 kinds.
What changed
The robot runs from start to finish. One kind of mistake was hiding in 231 places, putting wrong amounts on invoices without any error. Now the team can fix each kind everywhere at once.

For engineers Migration between automation platforms · 20 defects → 9 defect classes · one class with 231 instances of silent data corruption that passed functional tests.

Global mining company · 2026

7versions delivered, and three of five parts now being tested by the people who will use them

BOwnedI designed it and was responsible for it working

The mining company flooded with repair requests.

The problem
Workers send repair requests all day, in English and Spanish. Many are unclear or sent twice, and supervisors were spending time on requests that should never have reached them.
What I did
I designed a system where AI agents check how clear each request is and spot repeats, even in different words. A supervisor still gives the final yes before any repair is ordered.
What changed
Seven versions delivered. Three of the five parts are being tested by the real supervisors, who now have their own dashboard.

For engineers Solution architecture and design documents · LLM quality scoring · semantic duplicate detection (reject / flag / approve) · human-in-the-loop approval · SAP integration.

Global media agency · 2026

18page plain-language guide, so business and governance teams could understand the AI too

CAdvisedI guided the team that builds it

The ad planner who talks to an AI.

The problem
Planners split a client’s advertising money between TV, online, print and video, and they usually start from scratch, by hand.
What I did
I designed how an AI agent writes the first plan, then changes it when the planner simply says what they want, like “move money from print to video, keep the total”.
What changed
A partner company is building it, based on the architecture I proposed, the technical details and the guide I wrote.

For engineers Agent architecture · human feedback loop · revisions that respect constraints (a fixed total) · technical annex for the implementation partner.

Three things I’ve learned.

“Working” is not the same as “right”.The most dangerous mistake I found never showed an error. It quietly put wrong amounts on invoices.
Fix the family, not just the bug.When 20 mistakes turn out to be 9 kinds, you fix each kind everywhere at once.
Keep a person in charge.AI agents do the sorting and drafting. A person makes the call wherever a wrong answer would be costly.
